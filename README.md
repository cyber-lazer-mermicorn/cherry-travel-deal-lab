# Cherry Travel Deal Lab

**Employment and service lane** — systematic travel deal research, comparison, and portfolio-ready recommendations.

## 1. What this project does
Finds, compares, verifies, and packages travel opportunities into clear, actionable recommendations suitable for clients or a professional portfolio.

## 2. Who it is for
Potential clients, travel employers, and portfolio reviewers.

## 3. What exists today
Foundation structure and process definition.

## 4. What is planned
A Honolulu-origin trip comparison with full cost, restrictions, sources, and recommendation as the first proof.

## 5. What it does not claim
Does not book or sell tickets on behalf of others without proper licensing. Research and recommendation only unless otherwise stated.

## 6. First proof artifact
Honolulu-origin multi-option trip comparison package (three options, full cost, restrictions, source timestamps, recommended choice, backup choice).

## 7. Shared Mermicorn services
- [mermicorn-commerce-ai](https://github.com/cyber-lazer-mermicorn/mermicorn-commerce-ai)
- [mermicorn-token-saver](https://github.com/cyber-lazer-mermicorn/mermicorn-token-saver)
- [mermicorn-memory](https://github.com/cyber-lazer-mermicorn/mermicorn-memory)
- [mermicorn-mega-boot](https://github.com/cyber-lazer-mermicorn/mermicorn-mega-boot)

## 8. Information that remains private
Client data and any booking credentials live in mermicorn-private-ops or the private vault.
