# Cherry Travel Deal Lab

Employment and service lane for systematic travel deal research and portfolio-ready recommendations.

## What this project does
Finds, compares, verifies, and packages travel opportunities into clear, actionable recommendations suitable for clients or a professional portfolio.

## Why it exists
Cherry’s standout skill is spotting great deals — especially travel. This lab turns that talent into structured, evidence-based deliverables that support a travel-agent or deal-research career path.

## Working now
Foundation structure and process definition.

## Planned
Honolulu-origin trip comparison with full cost, restrictions, sources, and recommendation as first proof.

## Does not claim
Does not book or sell tickets on behalf of others without proper licensing. Research and recommendation only unless otherwise stated.
